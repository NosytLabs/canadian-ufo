    What the geography shows

    • The Maritimes dominate the famous cases. Nova Scotia holds two of the eighteen, and the two most-documented in the country. New Brunswick holds four, mostly modern witness reports rather than official files.
    • Newfoundland and Labrador is the aviation cluster. Gander 1951, Gander 1974 and Stephenville 2013 are all airborne; the last two involved multiple aircraft and were filed as vital-information reports, the kind of record that survives.
    • The north has the modern intercepts. The Yukon marker is the 11 February 2023 NORAD downing. not a mystery sighting, but the clearest instance of a Canadian UAP being tracked, intercepted and recovered.
    • The Prairies hold the strangest one. Falcon Lake is the only case in this index with a documented physical effect on a person that remains unexplained.

    How to read the colours

      A marker is a place, not a proof. Each circle is a case file with a source list; the grade tells you how hard you will have to work to stand behind it.
