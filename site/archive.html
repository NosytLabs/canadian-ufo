Archival index · 207 descriptions · 4 record groups

The whole index, in one table

Library and Archives Canada holds roughly 9,500 digitized documents on UFOs, accumulated between 1947 and the early 1980s by four federal bodies: the Department of National Defence, the Department of Transport, the National Research Council and the RCMP. What follows is that database's own index — all 207 descriptions, sortable, with a direct link into the live record for every entry.

This is an index, not the documents. Many entries carry a title and a record number and nothing else: no date, no location. That sparseness is itself a finding about how the file was built, and it is why roughly half the descriptions have no usable date.

03

Query the live database yourself

The LAC database is public and browsable. These are the query patterns that work — by department, or by province.

  • Browse by record group — National Research Council
    ?q7=National+Research+Council — the record-group term. Also works for Department of National Defence, Department of Transport, Royal Canadian Mounted Police.
  • Browse by province — New Brunswick
    ?q4=NB — the province or territory field. NB, NS, NL, PE, MB, ON, QC, BC, YT all return records.
  • Curated guide — 1967 Shag Harbour sighting and related research
    LAC's own research guide assembles the Shag Harbour material, with volume and microfilm references. This is the single best starting point for a specific case.
  • LAC scope page — Canada's UFOs: the search for the unknown
    Confirms four origin departments, 1947 to the early 1980s, ~9,500 digitized documents, and states plainly that dates are unstandardised and only about half the records name a location.
