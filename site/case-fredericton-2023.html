Fredericton

26 July 2023 NB Hearsay 45.95, -66.65 view on map

CBC reported a New Brunswick man convinced aliens were walking in the provincial capital, on the same week as the United States' first UAP hearing in Congress.

The record

The story is here as a document of the news cycle rather than an aviation or archival event. The witness reported seeing figures he believed to be non-human in a residential neighbourhood; CBC's coverage is explicit that no physical evidence was found and that the report came from a single individual. It represents the category of sighting that the Sky Canada Project report notes generates the most public confusion: a reported observation that enters the media record without any formal intake process.

Stanton Friedman, the physicist who became the most prominent public advocate for the extraterrestrial hypothesis, made Fredericton his home in 1980 and was a source for dozens of local and national stories until his death in 2019. His papers are held by the Provincial Archives of New Brunswick (PANB). Whether they include unpublished sighting reports from the region is an open question; the PANB has confirmed the collection exists but it has not been reviewed for this index.

The same week saw CBC's New Brunswick bureau cover a separate drone encounter in the province — a more concrete category of report involving a commercial pilot and Transport Canada's occurrence system. That case is filed separately as the 2023 New Brunswick report. The juxtaposition in the coverage illustrates how the same week can produce both a verifiable aviation occurrence and a purely anecdotal account that share a news cycle.
