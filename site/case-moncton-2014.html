Moncton

29 December 2014 NB Lights 46.09, -64.77 view on map

Community report, not an official file

Three blinking lights in a diagonal pattern over Moncton on a December evening, recorded on video and photographs by a witness and reported to a UFO society.

The record

Reported at roughly 7 p.m. on 29 December 2014 with both video and still photographs. The footage was published by CBC New Brunswick and CBC's national website picked it up, which is what distinguishes this from most civilian sighting reports: the video circulated widely enough to draw comment from Transport Canada. No CADORS occurrence was filed, which is consistent with a ground-based civilian report rather than a pilot or ATC filing.

The diagonal blinking pattern is what made the footage notable. A single light might be dismissed as an aircraft; three in formation, blinking in sequence and holding position, is harder to explain as a known navigation light configuration. The clip is short enough that altitude, speed and distance remain indeterminate from the footage alone.

The Sky Canada Project report's core finding is that most civilian sightings in Canada go unreported to any federal body. This case — video evidence, CBC coverage, Transport Canada comment, no CADORS filing, no analysis — is a precise illustration of that gap. The footage is in the public domain and has not been conclusively explained.
